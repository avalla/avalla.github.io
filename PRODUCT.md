# Product

## Register

brand

## Users

Engineering leaders, experienced technical interviewers, and recruiters at strong product companies. They need to assess seniority, product judgment, technical scope, and role fit quickly on the web or from an ATS-submitted PDF.

## Product Purpose

Present Andrea Valla credibly as a Staff-level Product Engineer with deep full-stack, architecture, systems, and production ownership experience. The CV should make scope, decisions, and outcomes legible before technology keywords, while remaining factual and easy to scrutinize.

## Brand Personality

Technical, assured, and direct. The tone is concise, factual, senior, product-oriented, and architectural.

## Anti-references

Avoid framework-first resumes, keyword walls, inflated titles, startup hype, generic corporate prose, AI-generated phrasing, decorative portfolio gimmicks, and visual treatments that weaken ATS or print readability.

## Design Principles

- Lead with ownership, architecture, and shipped systems.
- Use evidence and concrete scope instead of adjectives.
- Make the first 15 seconds communicate 20+ years, product ownership, technical leadership, and current TypeScript/React/Node.js/PostgreSQL/AI relevance.
- Preserve the site's existing identity while removing content and layout that compete with the positioning.
- Keep one maintainable content source for the web, console, metadata, and PDF export.

## Accessibility & Inclusion

Use semantic headings and text, readable contrast and type sizes, keyboard-accessible controls, meaningful link labels, responsive layouts, and an unclipped A4 print layout suitable for ATS parsing. Do not rely on color, images, or motion to communicate important information.
